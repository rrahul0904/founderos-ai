# Product specification

## Positioning

FounderOS is an evidence-first product operating system. It should help a founder decide what deserves to be built, preserve why decisions were made, and connect the deployed product back to the evidence that created it.

## Target customer

Initial ICP: solo technical founders and teams of 2–10 who currently switch among general AI chat, research tools, docs, issue trackers, coding agents, GitHub, hosting, analytics, and error monitoring.

## Jobs to be done

1. Turn a raw idea into falsifiable assumptions.
2. Gather evidence without confusing generated prose with verified facts.
3. Decide whether to proceed, narrow, pivot, or kill.
4. Produce an implementation-ready MVP specification.
5. Maintain architectural and product decision history.
6. Connect production signals to the next product decision.

## MVP wedge

The defensible wedge is not an all-in-one browser IDE. It is the **product brain**: evidence, decisions, context, and learning history that coding tools usually discard.

## North-star metric

Weekly evidence-backed decisions per active project.

## Guardrail metrics

- % of generated claims with provenance when external research is used.
- user override/rejection rate for agent decisions.
- median cost per evidence-backed decision.
- time from idea to approved MVP spec.
